# Privacy policy: Comments Exporter for Instagram

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Comments Exporter for Instagram is a browser extension that saves the comments of an Instagram post
to a file on your computer. This page explains what it does with data.

## What the extension reads

- The comments and replies on the post you open, with the public details Instagram shows next to
  them: username, profile link, profile picture link, verified badge, likes and date.
- When you run the follow check, whether each of those people follows the account that posted.

It requests this from instagram.com with the Instagram session that is already in your browser, the
same way the Instagram page does. It never reads, asks for, or stores your password.

## Where the data goes

- The loaded comments stay in the memory of the Instagram tab until you close or reload it.
- They are written to a file only when you click Download. Your browser saves that file to your
  computer.
- Your display choices (which columns, which file format, whether to include replies) are kept in
  the browser's local storage for instagram.com so they are remembered next time.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. The extension has no server.
- It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and loads no code from outside its package.
- It does not sell or share data.

## Your responsibilities

The files you export contain other people's comments and usernames. You are responsible for how you
store and use them, including under data-protection law and Instagram's terms.
